Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Student Hub Portal.

Question Answer
How do I register on the portal? Click on the Register page, fill in your details and submit the registration form.
How can I log in? Use your registered email ID and password on the Login page.
Can I update my profile? Yes. Open the Profile page after logging in and edit your information.
Where can I see upcoming events? Visit the Events page to view workshops, competitions and other college activities.
How do I contact the college? You can use the Contact page to send your message or enquiry.
Is Student Hub available on mobile? Yes. The portal can be accessed through any modern web browser on mobile or desktop.
